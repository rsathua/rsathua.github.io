Rishik Sathua

I am a Masters student at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, advised by Dr. Katherine Driggs-Campbell. My research is focused on efficiently modeling large datasets to be applied to real world unstructured generalizable robot systems. Prior to my Graduate studies, I worked for 2 years with Dr. Saurabh Gupta.
